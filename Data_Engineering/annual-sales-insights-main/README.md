Sales Annual  Project
Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing store sales data and generating a detailed Annual Sales Report.
The objective is to highlight sales trends, customer demographics, and business performance over the year.

The workflow included:

Data Cleaning

Data Processing

Data Analysis

Visualization with Excel (Pivot Tables & Dashboards)

Steps Involved
🔹 1. Data Cleaning

Checked and handled null/missing values.

Standardized gender values (m → Men, w → Women).

Converted categorical numbers in the Quantity column (one → 1, two → 2).

🔹 2. Data Processing

Age Group Classification

=IF(F2>=50,"Senior",IF(F2>=30,"Adult","Teenager"))


Extracted Month from Date

🔹 3. Data Analysis

Using Pivot Tables and aggregations, we analyzed:

Monthly & yearly sales trends

Customer demographics (age, gender)

Top-selling items and delivery status

Regional sales performance

Sales channels contribution

Key Insights
📊 1. Monthly Sales Trends

March recorded the highest sales, indicating seasonal demand.

👩‍🦰 2. Customer Demographics

Women dominated purchases, especially adult women.

🚚 3. Delivery Insights

Most orders were delivered successfully, showing an efficient logistics system.

🌍 4. Regional Performance

Top 5 states contributed the majority of sales → focus for targeted campaigns.

🛒 5. Sales Channels

Amazon contributed 35 sales, making it a strong sales channel.
